# Highland Park photo credits

Every photograph below comes from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and is
Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. Images were resized to ~900px wide and re-encoded as JPEG;
no other modifications were made. Attribution is reproduced here to satisfy the CC BY / CC BY-SA terms.

Species that Highland shares with Frick Park or Schenley Park (Canada Goldenrod, New England Aster,
Jewelweed, White Snakeroot, Giant Puffball, Dryad's Saddle) reuse the existing photographs in
`public/images/plants/`, `public/images/fungi/` and `public/images/schenley/` and are credited in
those files.

## Notes

- **CC BY-SA** images (share-alike) require that any modified version of the *image itself* be released
  under the same license. Using them unmodified inside the game, with the credit above, is fine.
- **CC0 / Public domain** images carry no attribution requirement; they are credited here anyway.
- The Chicory photograph is by a photographer in Cross Lanes, West Virginia, about four hours down
  the road, and the plant is the same roadside weed that lines the reservoir loop.
- The Pawpaw photograph shows the maroon flowers on a bare branch over a river, which is exactly the
  situation the species is in at the bottom of the Allegheny slope.
