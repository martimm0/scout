## Notes

- **CC BY-SA** images (share-alike) require that any modified version of the *image itself* be released
  under the same license. Using them unmodified inside the game, with the credit above, is fine.
- **CC0 / Public domain** images carry no attribution requirement; they are credited here anyway.
- The Dryad's Saddle file is titled with the synonym _Polyporus squamosus_; this is the same species as
  _Cerioporus squamosus_.
- Three images (Jack-o'-Lantern, Dryad's Saddle, Eastern Destroying Angel) originate from
  [Mushroom Observer](https://mushroomobserver.org) and are re-published on Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  Their Commons `Artist` field is a sentence rather than a bare name; the photographer's name has been
  extracted above and the Mushroom Observer origin is noted for the Jack-o'-Lantern, whose credit line
  names the source explicitly.
