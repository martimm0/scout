# Schenley Park photo credits

Every photograph below comes from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and is
Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. Images were resized to ~900px wide and re-encoded as JPEG;
no other modifications were made. Attribution is reproduced here to satisfy the CC BY / CC BY-SA terms.

Species that Schenley shares with Frick Park (Common Milkweed, New England Aster, Jewelweed,
Spicebush, Turkey Tail, Chicken of the Woods) reuse the existing photographs in
`public/images/plants/` and `public/images/fungi/` and are credited in those files.

- **CC0 / Public domain** images carry no attribution requirement; they are credited here anyway.

  from the park it is standing in for.
